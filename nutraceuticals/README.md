## The four things that matter

1. **Sell it as food, not as a supplement.** Drives VAT treatment, regulatory burden, platform access and
   channel fit. Must be designed into the first label — it cannot be retrofitted. Needs a specialist VAT
   opinion before artwork sign-off.
2. **Lead with green banana flour, not moringa or turmeric.** It is the supplier's actual specialism, it is
   differentiated in UK retail, and at ~65% resistant starch it carries a **lawful authorised health claim**
   — rare in this category.
3. **Gate Zero is the supplier's organic certification.** The catalogue evidences FSSAI, ISO 22000, HACCP and
   GMP but names no organic body. Without certification from a body on the Defra GB-recognised list, the
   product cannot be sold as organic in Great Britain. One email answers this.
4. **Test every consignment at an accredited third-party lab.** The UK applies extra controls to Indian
   imports for ethylene oxide and heavy metals. A rejected container is a write-off this budget cannot absorb.
