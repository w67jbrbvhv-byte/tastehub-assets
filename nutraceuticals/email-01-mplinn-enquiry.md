Dear MpLinn Foods team,

I am writing from Taste Hub Ltd, a UK food and drink importer and distributor
based in Southend-on-Sea. We have been trading since 2013, supplying the
wholesale, convenience and specialist retail channels in the United Kingdom.

We are developing a private label range for the UK market and your green banana

To move this forward I need the information below. I appreciate it is a detailed

1. ORGANIC CERTIFICATION — please answer these first

Your catalogue lists FSSAI, ISO 22000, HACCP and GMP, but does not name an
organic certification body. For us to sell any product as organic in Great
Britain, we need the following:

  a) A scan of your organic certificate, showing the certification body name,
     the body's code, the certified scope (which products and which operations),
     and the issue and expiry dates.

  b) Confirmation of whether your processing and packing facility is itself
     within the organic certification scope, or whether only the farms are
     certified.

  c) Whether your certification body can issue a GB Certificate of Inspection
     for each consignment shipped to Great Britain, endorsed by them.

  d) If you are not currently certified, please say so directly — it does not
     end our interest. Please tell us whether you are in conversion, with which
     body, and the expected date.

Please note that Great Britain operates its own list of recognised certification
bodies, which is separate from the EU list, so we do need the body's name and
code rather than a general statement of organic status.


2. PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

We would like to quote on the following, in retail-ready packs:

  A) Green Banana Flour (fine)
  B) Green Banana Powder (raw)
  C) Organic Green Banana Powder — subject to Section 1

For each, please send a full technical data sheet including:

  - Resistant starch content, expressed as a percentage of total starch
  - Total starch, dietary fibre, protein and moisture content
  - Particle size / mesh

  - Shelf life and storage conditions
  - Allergen statement, and details of any cross-contamination controls given

The resistant starch figure is important to us commercially, so please give the
actual tested value rather than a typical or literature figure, and confirm it
can be verified by independent analysis.


3. COMMERCIAL TERMS

  - Minimum order quantity per SKU, in finished retail units
  - Price per finished retail unit at three volumes: 2,000 / 5,000 / 10,000

  - Your nominated port of loading and typical sailing schedule to a UK port
  - Lead time from artwork approval to shipment
  - Payment terms you can offer

On payment terms, I should flag our requirement now rather than later: we work
on a deposit at order, with the balance payable on receipt of passing
certificates of analysis from an accredited third-party laboratory. Please
confirm whether you are able to work on that basis.

Our initial order would be in the low thousands of retail units per SKU, with
the intention to scale on reorder. If your standard MOQ is higher than that, we
are open to paying a higher unit price on a smaller first run against committed
volume on reorder — please quote on that basis if it helps.


4. PRIVATE LABEL AND PACKAGING

  - Retail pouch formats and sizes available
  - Pouch materials available, and whether a recyclable mono-material option
    exists
  - Whether you print labels in-house, and whether you can print to artwork we
    supply without alteration
  - Confirmation that you can print a UK-format label including our company
    name and address, ingredients, allergens in bold, best-before date, batch
    code, net quantity, and storage and usage instructions

Two process points we would need to agree:

  - Before any production printing, we require a physical printed proof,
    photographed and sent to us, and approved by us in writing. We do not

  - Before the container ships, we require photographs of the first filled and

5. TESTING AND QUALITY

Your catalogue mentions in-house lab testing alongside third-party COA. Could
you clarify which tests are done in-house and which by an external laboratory,

For every consignment we require batch-specific certificates from an accredited

  - Heavy metals: lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury
  - Pesticide multi-residue screen
  - Aflatoxins and mycotoxins

Please also confirm you retain reference samples from each batch for at least

## Why the email is built this way

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| Credentials in the first paragraph | A named UK importer trading since 2013 gets a faster reply and better pricing than an anonymous enquiry. |
| Green banana only, stated explicitly | Signals a specific, serious buyer rather than a catalogue tourist. Also keeps the botanical SKUs we have excluded out of the conversation entirely — there is nothing to gain from explaining UK novel food law to a supplier. |
| Section 1 marked as blocking, with an explicit "it does not end our interest" | Makes it safe for them to admit they are not certified. A supplier who fears losing the deal may fudge the answer, which is far worse for us than a straight no. |
| GB list flagged as separate from the EU list | Pre-empts the most likely reply — an NPOP or APEDA certificate offered as sufficient. It is not. |
| Resistant starch requested as a tested value, not a literature figure | It is the basis of our on-pack claim and has to be defensible. The commercial reasoning is not explained — that is our advantage to keep. |
| Payment on passing CoA raised now | If it is a dealbreaker, we need to know before spending on brand and artwork, not at PO stage. |
| MOQ flexibility offered upfront | Gives them a way to say yes to a small first run without losing face on their standard terms. |
| Paid sample, explicitly not free | Gets a representative sample rather than a hand-picked one, and marks us out from the free-sample traffic they will be used to. |
| Partial replies invited | A 25-question email gets ignored. This raises the odds of getting Section 1 answered quickly, which is the only part that gates spend. |
| Nothing disclosed about budget, pricing, margins or channel plan | Standard first-contact discipline. |

## Follow-up

- **No reply in 5 working days** → short chase to both addresses, then try WhatsApp on +91 9348526275. Indian exporters often respond considerably faster there than by email.
- **Reply dodges Section 1** → treat as a soft no on organic. Move to Route A (drop the organic claim, sell on provenance) and carry on qualifying them on everything else.
- **Reply names a certification body** → verify it against the Defra GB register before replying further:
  `gov.uk/government/publications/organic-registers-lists-of-third-countries-or-territories-control-bodies-and-control-authorities`
- Log all answers in the response tracker at the foot of `supplier-rfq.md`.
