# 12-Month Launch Plan

Phase one. Two SKUs, trade-first, no paid media. Every phase ends in a gate; a failed gate stops spend
rather than escalating it.

---

## Month 0–1 — Qualify (spend: ~£2,000)

**The only objective is to find out whether this business is possible.** No inventory, no artwork, no brand.

| Task | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Send `supplier-rfq.md` to MpLinn Foods | TH | Written responses |
| Verify their organic body against the Defra GB list | TH | Pass / fail |
| Order paid full-specification sample | TH | Physical sample |
| Independent UK lab test of sample — full panel + resistant starch | ADV | CoA |
| Engage food-VAT specialist for written opinion | ADV | Written opinion |
| Register as Food Business Operator with Southend-on-Sea City Council | TH | Registration (free) |

### 🚦 Gate 0 — Organic
Certificate from a Defra GB-recognised body covering **farms and packing site**?
→ **Yes:** Route B, retain organic. → **No:** Route A, drop organic, sell on provenance.
*Neither answer stops the project. Not knowing does.*

### 🚦 Gate 1 — VAT
Specialist confirms zero-rating is defensible?
→ **No:** re-run the economics at 20% VAT. If margin fails, **stop here** — before inventory.

### 🚦 Gate 2 — Supplier
Independent CoA clean and agreeing with supplier's? Resistant starch ≥14% of total starch?
→ **No:** qualify an alternative supplier before proceeding.

## Month 3–4 — Commit (spend: ~£11,500)

### 🚦 Gate 3 — Trade validation
**Before placing the production order:** at least 2 wholesaler listings agreed, or 15 direct accounts
committed.
→ **No:** cut to 1 SKU and reduce the first run. Do not place a full order on hope.

| Task | Owner |
|---|---|
| Printed proof received and photographed | SUP |
| **Pre-production gate:** written sign-off by regulatory and VAT advisers | ADV |
| Written authorisation to print issued | TH |
| Place production order — 2 SKUs | TH |
| Pre-shipment third-party testing | SUP |
| Photographs of first filled units received | SUP |
| Payment released **only on passing CoAs** | TH |
| Book freight; prepare customs entry with CETA preference claim | TH |

*Production 7–15 business days; sea freight ~4–5 weeks. Plan for stock landing in month 5.*

## Month 6–9 — Sell in

Focus is entirely on distribution, not on consumer marketing.

| Task | Owner |
|---|---|
| Convert wholesaler listings to first orders | TH |
| Direct account calls — delis, farm shops, specialist convenience | TH |
| Organic content: recipes, provenance, the gut-health story | TH |
| TikTok Shop — organic content and creator sampling, no paid spend | TH |
| Collect reviews and repeat-purchase data | TH |

**Track:** accounts opened, units per account, sell-through rate. **Not** revenue — revenue lags listing by
six to eight weeks and will look alarming if watched too early.

---

## Month 9–12 — Prove and decide

### 🚦 Gate 4 — Reorder
Does sell-through support a second container within six months of landing?

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | Place second order. Add SKU 3 (turmeric or masala blend). Release contingency to test paid social against measured repeat rate. |
| **Marginal** | Reorder the stronger SKU only. Fix the weaker one's channel or proposition before spending more. |
| **No** | Do not scale. Reassess channel mix and pricing before committing further capital. |

Also review at month 12:

- Second supplier qualified? (Single-source dependency on a company founded 2024 is the standing structural risk.)
- Amazon revisit — trade mark now registered; is a differentiated blend ready?
- Packaging EPR threshold check against actual turnover and tonnage.
